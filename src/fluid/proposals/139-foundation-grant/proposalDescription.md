# Increase Foundation Monthly Grant from $250,000 to $350,000

This proposal increases the Fluid Foundation's monthly grant from $250,000 to $350,000 and executes the first disbursement at the new rate. It delegates the Fluid timelock into payload `0xe9941Bb0844160BdE0Cc19f7C0F0357D4Af78400`, whose `execute()` action withdraws exactly 155 stETH from the Fluid Reserve to the Fluid Foundation.
